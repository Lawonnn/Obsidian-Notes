Frustration is an emotional reaction to an event, situation, or condition that occurs in the form disappointment or powerlessness. 
There are 2 types of frustration 
- One is caused by **external influences**, such as negatives opinions of superiors
- **Inner frustration**, caused by conscious or somewhat subconscious thought process

> [!reminder]
> Feelings `reflect subconscious thoughts` and thought processes

> [!tip]
> Often it helps to listen to our thoughts from a 3rd-person perspective or imagine that our best friend expresses these thoughts. With that, we gain some distance from the feeling of being affected by it, which makes it easier to construct an objective opinion and judgment about it.
