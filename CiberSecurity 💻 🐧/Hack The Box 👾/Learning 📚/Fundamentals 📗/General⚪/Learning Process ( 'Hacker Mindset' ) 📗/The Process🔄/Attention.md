- Attention is an independent mental process that takes places subconsciously. So when we talk about concentration, we mean the maintenance of our attention on a specific topic
- Attention goes hand in hand with concentration and focus
- Our attention will begin to decrease at some point, and we will no longer be able to absorb information effectively. We are getting stuck at this point, forcing ourselves to keep trying, and learning ends up with `problems` of understanding and, therefore, with higher `frustration`.

> [!important]
> Make sure that you feel comfortable and ready to learn new things.
