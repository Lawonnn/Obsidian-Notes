**Key Concepts:**

- **Information Security (Infosec):** The practice of protecting data from unauthorized access, use, or disruption. It is centered around the `CIA triad`: protecting the **C**onfidentiality, **I**ntegrity, and **A**vailability of data.
- **Risk Management Process:** A five-step cycle organizations follow to handle threats: `Identifying`, `Analyzing`, `Evaluating`, `Dealing with`, and `Monitoring` risks. This ensures security measures are effective without harming productivity.
- **Red Team vs. Blue Team:** Two fundamental roles in security. The **Red Team** simulates attackers to find weaknesses (offensive security), while the **Blue Team** builds and manages defenses to protect the organization (defensive security).

**Practical Application:**

- Penetration testers are a type of Red Team role. They are hired to identify, reproduce, and report security risks within an organization's networks and applications. Their work is a critical part of an organization's overall risk management strategy.
